# Master Channel Bible: 《BoomBoom English : Toby & Momo》

## 1. Brand Identity, Worldview & The 15-Second Rule Gadget
* **Official Channel Title:** BoomBoom English : Toby & Momo
* **1.5-Second Sonic Brand Stinger (Placed at 0:13–0:15 after Cold Open):**
  * `[Audio: Two punchy Taiko/Timpani Booms — BOOM! BOOM! + Xylophone glissando]`
  * `[Voiceover: "Boom-Boom English!" -> Toby & Momo jump in: "Yeah!"]`
* **Worldview (Wonder Paw Town):** A colorful coastal town where animal squad members and human kids solve whimsical everyday mysteries and giant slapstick accidents.
* **Core Pedagogical Gadget — "The Word-Pop Watch":**
  * Worn on Toby's left wrist. Shoots a magical restoration beam to fix any problem in town, but is 100% voice-activated: it only fires when the Target English Phrase is spoken clearly 3 times.
  * **The Slapstick Misfire Rule:** Whenever Momo impatiently shouts the wrong English word or mispronounces it (e.g., saying "Big banana!" instead of "Small!"), the watch misfires with a comical Wah-Wah! horn and drops a giant banana cream pie or bubble cloud onto Momo's head.
