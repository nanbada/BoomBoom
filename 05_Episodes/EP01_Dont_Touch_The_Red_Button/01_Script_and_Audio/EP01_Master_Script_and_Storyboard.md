## 2. Act I: Establishing the "Word-Pop Watch" Rule & Attempt #1 (0:15 – 1:15)

### 0:15 – 0:40 (Fixing the Giant Strawberry — Exposures #3 & #4)
* **Visual:** Momo is stuck under the giant strawberry, wiggling his feet. Toby points his Word-Pop Watch at the strawberry. A hologram icon of a tiny strawberry (SMALL) pops up above the watch.
* **Audio:**
  * `[SFX: Hologram Pop! -> 0.3s clean window]`
  * TOBY: *"Don't worry, Momo! My Word-Pop Watch can fix it! Say the magic words with me: (Exposure #3:) Make it... [0.5s pause] ...SMALL!"*
  * MOMO (Wiggling): *"(Exposure #4:) Make it... SMALL!"*
  * `[SFX: Magical laser zing! -> Deflating balloon whistle]` — The strawberry shrinks back to normal size and pops into Momo's mouth!

### 0:40 – 1:15 (Attempt #1 Escalation — Momo Touches It Again! Exposures #5 & #6)
* **Visual:** Toby turns around to clean up: *"Remember, Momo! (Exposure #5:) Don't... touch it!"*
* **Visual Pre-Cue (0.0s):** A pesky fly lands right on the Red Button. Momo tries to swat the fly ("Shoo! Shoo!") and accidentally body-slams the Red Button again!
* **Visual Impact:** The purple beam shoots out the window and hits Barnaby the Koala, who is sleeping on a bench outside. Barnaby inflates into a HOUSE-SIZED GIANT KOALA snoring loudly!
* **Audio (+0.3s after Boing):**
  * MOMO (Eyes +20% enlarged, jaw dropped): *"Meow-my gosh! Look at Barnaby! (Exposure #6:) He is... too... BIG!"*

## 3. Act II: The 120 BPM "Word-Pop Chant" & Banana Pie Misfire (1:15 – 2:15)

### 1:15 – 1:40 (Mid-Point Retention Booster — 120 BPM Musical Chant, Exposures #7 & #8)

### 1:40 – 2:15 (The Slapstick Misfire Gag)
* **Visual:** Toby aims the Word-Pop Watch at Giant Sleeping Barnaby.
* **Audio:** TOBY: *"Quick, Momo! Say 'Make it SMALL!'"*
