# EP01: Don't Touch the Red Button! — Master Production Script & Audio Plan

* **Target Age:** 5–7 Years (Tier A Starter Track)
* **Target English Phonics & Keywords:**
  * Core Imperatives: `"Don't touch it!"`, `"Push the button!"`, `"Stop!"`
  * Antonym & Vocabulary: `"Red vs. Blue"`, `"Big vs. Small"`, `"Button"`, `"Dance"`
* **Total Duration:** 04:30 (4 Minutes 30 Seconds)
* **Master Rules Enforced:**
  * Triple-Lock: `[0.0s Visual Pre-Cue] -> [0.8s Speech with 0.5s pause] -> [1.8s Subtitle + Echo]`
  * 60:25:15 Ratio: 60% Slapstick/Chase, 25% Dialogue (10x Repetitions), 15% Chant & Quiz
  * Zero-Logo Cold Open & Zero-Outro Cliffhanger

---

## Act-by-Act Production Breakdown

### 0:00 – 0:15 | Scene 1: Zero-Logo Cold Open & Sonic Stinger
* **[0:00] Visual:** Extreme close-up of a giant, glowing, pulsating red dome button sitting in the center of Detective HQ. A big yellow hazard sign hangs above it: `DON'T TOUCH!`.
* **[0:02] Action:** Momo creeps in on tiptoes, his pupils dilated to 120%. His paw trembles 2 millimeters from the button.
* **[0:05] Audio / Dialogue:**
  * Toby (leaps into frame, paws outstretched in panic): *"Momo, NO! ... [0.5s pause] ... **DON'T TOUCH IT!**"* `(Exposure #1)`
* **[0:07] VFX:** Elastic text pops up with 12px yellow glow: `DON'T TOUCH IT!`.
* **[0:10] Action:** Momo pulls his paw back and giggles nervously: *"Hehehe, okay, Toby! I won't touch it!"*
* **[0:13] Audio / Stinger:** `[BOOM-BOOM! Taiko hit] -> "Boom-Boom English! Yeah!"` *(Toby and Momo quick high-five into camera).*

---

### 0:15 – 1:15 | Scene 2 (Act I): Comedic Temptation & First Misfire
* **[0:15] Context:** Toby turns around to inspect the Big Clue Screen. The red button makes a tantalizing squeaky-toy sound.
* **[0:25] Action:** Momo's paw involuntarily creeps toward the button again. Toby turns his head with a whistle sound.
* **[0:30] Dialogue:**
  * Toby: *"Remember Momo! ... [0.5s pause] ... **Don't touch the RED button!**"* `(Exposure #2)`
  * Momo: *"What about the BLUE button?"* *(Momo points to a tiny blue button).*
  * Toby: *"The blue button is SMALL. The red button is BIG! ... [0.5s pause] ... **Don't touch the BIG red button!**"* `(Exposure #3)`
* **[0:45] Slapstick Climax:** Toby sneezes and closes his eyes for 1 second. Momo can't resist: *"I just want to poke it once!"* — *SLAP!* Momo pushes the red button.
* **[0:50] Chaos:** A siren blares! The floor vibrates! The red button triggers the "Dance Ray"!
* **[0:55] Action:** Momo's feet start dancing uncontrollably like rubber tentacles. Momo spins like a disco top, crashing into a stack of cardboard boxes: *"Whoaaa! My feet won't stop dancing! Help!"*

---

### 1:15 – 2:15 | Scene 3 (Act II): Escalation & 120 BPM Word-Pop Chant
* **[1:15] Context:** The dance ray hits the furniture! The detective sofa, the lamp, and Toby's magnifying glass start tap-dancing around HQ.
* **[1:25] Music Cue:** 120 BPM upbeat marimba and ukulele groove kicks in. Coco bunny bounces in wearing her pink hoodie.
* **[1:30 – 1:55] 120 BPM Micro-Chant (Exposures #4, #5, #6):**
  * *(Measure 1-2)* Coco & Toby: *"Red button, red button, round and bright!"*
  * *(Measure 3-4)* Chorus & Kids: *"Don't touch it! Stop! Don't touch it! Stop!"* `(Exposure #4)`
  * *(Measure 5-6)* Coco & Toby: *"Big red button, do you hear the sound?"*
  * *(Measure 7-8)* Momo (dancing silly): *"Don't touch it! Stop! Turn around and around!"* `(Exposure #5)`
  * *(Measure 9-10)* Full Chorus: *"1, 2, 3... DON'T TOUCH IT! FREEZE!"* `(Exposure #6)`
* **[2:00] Visual:** All dancing furniture freezes in mid-air in funny poses.

---

### 2:15 – 3:30 | Scene 4 (Act III): Climax Crisis & 4th-Wall Audience Rescue
* **[2:15] Crisis:** The frozen furniture wobbles! The red button flashes faster and faster: `DANGER! MAX DANCE ENERGY!`.
* **[2:25] Dialogue:**
  * Toby: *"We must turn off the red button! I will use the Word-Pop Watch!"*
* **[2:35] Slapstick Misfire:** Momo rushes in to help: *"I can say the magic words! ... Push the banana!"*
  * *Watch Audio:* `[Sad buzzer] -> [Splat SFX]`
  * *Visual:* The watch shoots a giant banana cream pie right onto Momo's face! Momo wipes cream from his whiskers: *"Pbbbbt! Not banana!"*
* **[2:50] 4th-Wall Direct Call-and-Response (Exposures #7, #8, #9):**
  * Toby turns directly to the camera, holding out his glowing Word-Pop Watch:
  * Toby: *"Friends at home, I need your help! The watch needs the magic phrase! Shout it with me 3 times!"*
  * *Call 1:* Toby: *"DON'T TOUCH IT!"* `(Exposure #7)` -> Watch fills 33% golden energy.
  * *Call 2 (Louder):* Toby: *"Again! DON'T TOUCH IT!"* `(Exposure #8)` -> Watch fills 66% golden energy.
  * *Call 3 (Full Shout):* Toby & Momo: *"ONE MORE TIME, LOUD! **DON'T TOUCH IT!**"* `(Exposure #9)` -> Watch hits 100% with star sparkles!
* **[3:20] Resolution:** Toby presses his watch. A radiant golden beam shoots out, striking the red button. The red button pops back up to its safe position with a gentle *Ding!*. All furniture lands safely on the carpet.

---

### 3:30 – 4:15 | Scene 5 (Act IV): Celebratory Dance & 30s Speed Detective Quiz
* **[3:30] Dialogue:**
  * Toby: *"Hooray! The red button is off!"*
  * Momo (hugging Toby): *"I will NEVER touch the red button again... maybe!"*
* **[3:45 – 4:15] 30-Second Speed Detective Quiz (Exposure #10):**
  * *Audio:* Fast clock ticking SFX (`25_Quiz_Clock_Tick_3s.wav`).
  * *Q1 (Color):* Two buttons appear on screen: Blue and Red.
    * Toby: *"Which one is the RED button? Point to it in 3... 2... 1!"* `[Ding!]` *(Red button lights up with gold stars).*
  * *Q2 (Size):* Giant Red Button vs. Tiny Red Button.
    * Momo: *"Which one is the BIG button? Point in 3... 2... 1!"* `[Ding!]` *(Big button bounces).*
  * *Q3 (Action):*
    * Toby: *"What do we say when someone wants to touch it? ... That's right! **DON'T TOUCH IT!**"* `(Exposure #10)`

### 4:15 – 4:30 | Scene 6 (Act V): Zero-Outro Cliffhanger
* **[4:15] Action:** Toby and Momo sit on the sofa eating apple slices. Everything is peaceful.
* **[4:20] Mystery Audio:** *THUMP... THUMP... THUMP!* A heavy footstep shakes the HQ window.
* **[4:25] Cliffhanger Visual:** A giant furry green claw slides under the doorway, leaving a glowing neon green footprint on the welcome mat. Toby gasps. Momo drops his apple.
* **[4:29] Toby (Looking at camera with wide eyes):** *"Uh-oh... Whose footprint is that?!"*
* **[4:30] Hard Cut to Black** *(Triggers immediate YouTube Autoplay to EP02)*.
