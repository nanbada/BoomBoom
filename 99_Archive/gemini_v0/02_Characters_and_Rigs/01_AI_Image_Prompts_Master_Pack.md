## 5. Midjourney Consistent Character Workflow Instructions
1. **Initial Seed Generation:** Run Prompt 1-1 (Toby) and Prompt 2-1 (Momo) to establish the primary character visual baseline. Pick the cleanest output and upscale.
2. **Character Reference Locking:** In subsequent generation passes for isolated limbs and visemes, append `--cref [URL_OF_UPSCALED_IMAGE] --cw 100` to preserve fur tones, line weights, and costume color codes verbatim.
3. **Style Reference Locking:** Append `--sref [URL_OF_UPSCALED_IMAGE]` to guarantee identical shading and vector line thickness across all 24 visemes.
4. **Vector Conversion:** Import generated PNGs into Adobe Illustrator or Vectorizer.ai to convert into clean, scale-independent SVG/AI vector paths before importing into Moho Pro 14 or Cartoon Animator 5 switch layers.
