## 2. Visual Design Golden Ratios: Balancing "Cute" vs. "Not Babyish" (Ages 5–10)
Children ages 8–10 reject 1.5-head-tall toddler blob designs. Characters must look agile, expressive, and ready for action.

* **Head-to-Body Ratio (Proportions):**
  * Toddler (0–3): 1.5 to 2.0 heads tall (chubby, limited limb articulation).
  * Target 5–10 Golden Ratio: 2.5 to 3.2 heads tall (Toby = 2.8 heads tall; Momo = 2.5 heads tall; Coco/Lex = 3.0–3.1 heads tall). Allows dynamic running, jumping, and detective poses.
* **Eye Size & Modular Pupils:**
  * Eyes occupy 25%–30% of the facial area, built with switchable vector pupil layers (normal, +20% shocked, starry-eyed, dizzy spirals).
* **The Silhouette "Shadow Test":**
  * Every character must be 100% recognizable when filled with solid black (Toby's floppy ears + detective cap; Momo's round cheeks + forehead goggles; Coco's tall arrow-like bunny ears).
* **The 3-Color Rule:**
  * Restrict each character to 3 signature flat colors (Primary Fur/Body + Secondary Outfit/Gear + High-Contrast Accent/Muzzle) with zero complex gradients so 2D bone joints rotate cleanly without visual seams.

## 3. EFL / English Education-Specific Design Mechanics
* **High-Contrast Muzzle Zone for Phonetic Lip-Sync:**
  * Always color the area immediately surrounding the character's mouth in a light, high-contrast shade (Pure White #FFFFFF or Cream) so dark mouth cavities, white teeth, and pink tongue positions stand out clearly for non-native learners.
* **12-Viseme Geometric Mouth Architecture:**
  * Include dedicated visual switches for tricky EFL sounds: TH (tongue protruding between teeth), F/V (upper teeth biting lower lip), L (tongue tip touching upper palate), and a +20% Oversized Keyword Shout Mouth.
* **Global Multi-Cultural Safety Checklist:**
  * *Neutral Attire:* Use universal hoodies, vests, caps, and sneakers; avoid country-specific school uniforms or religious symbols.
  * *Universal Props & Foods:* Use apples, carrots, pizza, ice cream, and bananas; avoid culturally restricted meats or taboos.
  * *Safe Gestures:* Stick to universal cartoon gestures (pointing, cupping ear, high-five, thumbs-up, shrugging).
