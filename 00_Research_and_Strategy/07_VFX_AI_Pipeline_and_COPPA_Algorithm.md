## 1. The 5 Core Lexical Highlighting & Pop-Up VFX Mechanics
1. **Elastic Pop-Up (Overshoot Bounce):** Flashcards/icons scale from 0% -> 115% -> 95% -> 100% over 12 frames (0.5s) using an elastic cubic-bezier curve + Pop! SFX.
2. **12px High-Contrast Outline Glow + 25% Background Dimming:** When a target noun is spoken, apply a 12px bright yellow/white outer glow around that object for 1.5s while dimming background layers by 25% opacity to eliminate referent ambiguity (Gavagai Problem).
3. **Phonetic Karaoke Text Sync:** Display 1–4 words in bold rounded sans-serif (Fredoka One). Each spoken syllable scales up by +20% and flips color from White to Bright Yellow (#FFD600).
4. **Kinetic Word-Physics:** Typography physically acts out its meaning (COLD shivers with ice crystals; JUMP leaps along a parabola; HEAVY drops and squashes flat).
5. **Mouth-to-Object PIP / 25% Snap-Zoom:** Zooms 25% onto the character's 12-viseme mouth during key pronunciation moments.

**Anti-Fatigue Camera & Transition Rule:** Keep the camera static during 80% of dialogue. Never use fade-to-black (kids think the video ended); use 0.6-second graphic wipes (bubbles, magnifying glass pass-through, or cartoon Iris-Out).
