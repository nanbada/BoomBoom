# [Research Module 4] High-CTR Thumbnail Engineering, Visual Psychology & Title Formulas (Target Ages: 5–10)

## 1. The Dual-Targeting Strategy: Kid Appeal (70%) vs. Parent Trust (30%)

| Target Persona | Core Desire & Triggers | What They Look For in a Thumbnail |
|---|---|---|
| The Child (5–10) [70% Weight] | Instant emotional stimulation, curiosity, slapstick humor, vibrant colors, familiar characters in extreme predicaments. | Extreme Facial Expressions & Visual Chaos: Wide eyes (+20% scale), comical shock, huge tears, unexpected props, or giant glowing objects occupying >50% of horizontal width. |
| The Parent / Educator [30% Weight] | Clean moral value, structured learning cues, high production quality, safety, and zero toxic/cringe content. | Clarity & Educational Signaling: Bright studio-grade vector lighting, minimal legible English text (1–3 words max), and a recognizable educational badge. |

## 2. The 4 Core Visual Design Rules for Thumbnails
1. **The Single-Focus Rule (Zero Clutter):** Never place more than 2 characters/objects in a thumbnail. The focal point must be decoded in under 0.5 seconds on a small mobile screen.
2. **High-Saturation Complementary Color Contrast:** Pair Electric Yellow (#FFD600) vs. Deep Royal Blue (#1E50FF) or Coral Orange (#FF6B2B) vs. Mint/Forest Green. Avoid muddy browns or low-contrast pastels.
3. **Exaggerated Facial Micro-Expressions:** Enlarge eyes by 15%–20% over standard animation models; use open gasping or laughing mouths and high-arched eyebrows.
4. **Out-of-Frame Gaze Vector:** Position the character's eyes or pointing finger directly toward the central mystery object or video title.

## 3. Top 5 High-CTR Thumbnail Layout Patterns
* **Pattern A: "Before & After" Scale Contrast (Split-Screen):** Vertical split showing a tiny frustrated character on the left vs. a giant house-sized object on the right.
* **Pattern B: "Mystery Silhouette / What's Inside?" Box:** A locked crate or glowing box in the center with furry ears peeking out and a giant ? above it.
* **Pattern C: "Disaster / Mess" Comic Consequence:** Momo covered head-to-toe in banana pie or colorful bubbles with spiral eyes.
* **Pattern D: Giant Object Intrusion (Scale Disruption):** Characters trapped inside a giant glass jar or running from a giant rolling watermelon.
* **Pattern E: "Hidden Clue / Detective Search" (Ages 7–10 Focus):** Toby holding a giant magnifying glass over a glowing footprint while grinning at the viewer.
